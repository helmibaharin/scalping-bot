# Strategy 5 — Volume Profile

## Repository audit and integration

The existing entry point is `MQL5/Experts/ScalpingBot.mq5`. Strategies 1–4 already have implemented 4H, Shape/legacy Triangle, ORB, and two SMT profile engines. `StrategyInterface.mqh` routes magic numbers/signals, `TradeExecutor.mqh` submits protected requests, `RiskManager.mqh` controls whole-account exposure, `PositionManager.mqh` manages only owned tickets, and `TradeLedger.mqh` durably joins actual orders/deals/positions. Session utilities previously centered risk accounting on New York days. Native MQL fixtures, C# adaptations of production rules/risk, Python reports and an isolated portable tester already exist.

Strategy 5 reuses that executor and risk budget. It has three internal strategy routing slots (5/6/7) under one top-level Volume Profile strategy. Its first-confirmation coordinator applies **only within S5**. Strategies 1–4 retain their signal rules, target calculations and existing execution ordering. The correction at the end of the request supersedes the earlier global one-position policy: a hedged S1–4 position does not consume S5's slot, and S5 does not impose a slot restriction on S1–4. The three VP methods share one position slot per symbol regardless of direction. Netting collisions block with `ACCOUNT_MODE_INCOMPATIBLE`; foreign exposure is never netted, reversed or modified. Shared account caps can still block any strategy.

The seven pre-existing `.set` files, including the user's all-four-strategy edits to the SMT files, are preserved byte for byte. New Volume Profile defaults are disabled in old presets. Parent project/CapitalGuard files are untouched. Preservation receipts compare against the snapshot taken at the beginning of this task, not against an older preset that predates the user's edits.

## Calculation and session selection

The source is completed broker M1 candles and **tick volume**, never exchange-wide volume or an implicit alternative timeframe. Price bins default to $0.10, anchored at integer multiples of the bin size from zero. Internally bins are half-open `[lower, upper)`. Positive-range candles allocate volume proportionally to overlap length under a uniform high–low density. An exact high edge contributes no volume to the following bin. Zero-range bars put their full volume in the containing integer-indexed bin. Configured bin size must be an integer multiple of the symbol's trading tick size; POC is the selected bin's center. SL/TP execution prices are separately rounded to valid trading ticks.

POC maximizes allocated volume. Equal-volume bins are compared with a documented numeric tolerance of `max(1e-10, total_volume*1e-12)`; choose nearest to the allocated-volume weighted center, then the lower bin. Value Area starts at POC and repeatedly adds one immediately adjacent bin with greatest volume. Adjacent-volume ties prefer the candidate nearer the weighted center, then lower. Expansion remains contiguous until at least the configured 70% is included. VAL is the lowest included lower edge; VAH is the highest included upper edge. Profiles record total volume, weighted center, POC ties and validation status.

The reference is the latest positive-tick-volume completed broker D1 trading day before the current broker calendar day. Weekends/holidays with no trading D1 bar are skipped; a valid most recent D1 bar with missing M1 data blocks and retries rather than silently selecting an older day. A configurable maximum calendar lookback also rejects stale D1 history. The reference close is the last valid M1 close and must match D1 close.

Weekly broker trade sessions, including overnight windows, are unioned into a broker-day minute mask. M1 OHLC and summed tick volume must match the completed D1 OHLC/volume before a profile is accepted. This catches incomplete M1 history even where simple timestamp coverage looks adequate. Ordered unique minute timestamps, finite valid OHLC, nonnegative tick volume, a positive total, session completeness, bounded bins, volume conservation and ordered levels are checked.

MT5's weekday session API is a current weekly schedule, **not a historical holiday calendar**. `InpVPAllowD1VerifiedPartial=true` permits a documented `D1_VERIFIED_SESSION_EXCEPTION` when complete D1 OHLC/volume reconciliation proves that reported M1 data covers a historical partial session, interruption or changed schedule. At least the configured 60 M1 bars are still required. The raw weekly scheduled-minute count and ratio remain in diagnostics, even when below 95% or outside the current weekly schedule. They are not relabeled as 100% normal-session coverage. Turning this option off fails closed for such exceptions. Data where both broker D1 and M1 aggregates are wrong in the same way cannot be independently audited without another source; no external calendar is assumed.

Levels are frozen only after successful validation and a durable cache write. An atomic temporary-file move stores a checksummed snapshot with active day, reference session, calculation-parameter fingerprint and metadata. Restart reload validates date, parameters, checksum and level invariants. A corrupt cache or intraday parameter change blocks S5 rather than replacing frozen levels. Missing history is retried at the configured interval. The current unfinished day is excluded. Source buffers and histogram sizes are bounded; profile reconstruction occurs once per broker day/restart, M5 decisions once per completed candle, and broader context once per completed M15 candle.

The ATR convention is the arithmetic mean of the last 14 completed M5 true ranges (high-low versus gaps to previous close), recalculated at each decision. This explicit bounded-history convention is not Wilder exponential smoothing. The period is configurable; it never uses the unfinished M5 bar.

## Method states

**M1 — `S5_M1_POC_BOUNCE`.** Previous close above VAH selects BUY from a POC approach from above; below VAL selects SELL from below. Boundary-equal previous closes count as inside and disable M1. A fresh approach must start with a previous completed close beyond the configurable POC proximity zone, then interact with POC and reject on the expected side. Excessive penetration, wrong-side closes, expiry or overextension invalidate it. This prevents repeatedly recycling a lingering POC interaction.

A completed real-body engulfing confirmation uses opposite prior body and matching current direction, with optional tick-based equality tolerance, and selects 2R. Alternatively, two consecutive completed directional candles **after** the rejection must move away from POC and select 1.5R. A touch/rejection alone does not enter. If both raw flags apply, engulfing has explicit precedence. SL always uses POC minus the BUY buffer or plus the SELL buffer; the default buffer is 0.10 of the last completed M5 ATR. No wick stop substitutes for an invalid POC stop.

**M2 — `S5_M2_VALUE_AREA_REVERSAL`.** Only an inclusive inside-value reference close enables it. A fresh excursion below VAL or above VAH from a prior inside/at-boundary close arms BUY or SELL respectively. A completed close strictly between VAL and VAH confirms reentry; an exact boundary or a wick without an inside close does not. An unsequenced two-sided excursion is rejected. The stop is beyond an associated confirmed M5 excursion swing known before the entry candle, plus 0.10 ATR. The explicitly enabled fallback uses the most adverse extreme of already completed excursion candles when no such confirmed pivot exists; disable the fallback to reject those setups. A same-candle excursion and strict reentry may qualify through this completed-bar fallback. The target is 2R. Consumed excursions cannot be reused; a new outside excursion is required.

**M3 — `S5_M3_BREAKOUT_CONTINUATION`.** Independent of reference-close location. A new directional M5 close crossing VAH/VAL must reach 0.30 ATR displacement and at least 0.40 ATR real-body size. It then waits for a separate meaningful retracement of at least 0.10 ATR toward the boundary, within 0.30 ATR proximity. Both pullback body endpoints must remain outside/on the broken boundary. A small wick may enter value by at most 0.15 ATR. **Any body penetration is invalid**, including a tiny opening penetration followed by an outside close; 0.25 ATR is diagnostic only and never grants permission.

The pullback candle cannot provide its own subsequent BOS. Its protective low/high must become a confirmed 2-left/2-right M5 pivot before the BOS candle opens. A previously known relevant swing high/low, before/during the sequence and no older than the configured lookback, must then be crossed by a fresh completed close in the breakout direction. An already broken pre-breakout reference is not recycled as a new BOS. Required M15 context is calculated from completed confirmed structure; optional context alignment defaults off and introduces no hidden trend filter. An opposite confirmed context blocks only when that optional filter is enabled. SL is beyond the confirmed pullback extreme plus 0.10 ATR, TP 2R. Broken structure, inside body, excess wick, missing data or timeout invalidate; a new significant breakout is required. M3 failure never automatically creates M2 eligibility.

All methods record state transitions, reasons, ATR, levels and confirmation type. Simultaneous opposing earliest S5 signals skip all tied methods. Same-direction ties choose lowest method number after earliest confirmation time. Nonselected or risk-blocked confirmations are consumed, not queued for chasing after another position closes. All methods continue evaluating and logging while the slot is occupied.

## Risk, ownership and restart

Default new presets size by equity risk, not a fixed 0.01 lot: 0.25% requested per trade, 1% maximum combined account open risk and 2% daily equity loss. Actual risk calculations include configured commission/deviation allowance, broker contract/tick sizing, lot steps, stops/freeze, margins, spread, permissions and all other account positions. Below-minimum volume is rejected, not rounded upward. No SL widening substitutes for the strategy structure. RR 1.5 is allowed only through the new VP path; existing S1–4 target minimum policies remain unchanged. Fill risk is validated and the fixed-R target is repaired from actual entry where needed using the existing protected-ticket manager.

Three distinct magics are reserved after repository collision checks: M1 `26103801`, M2 `26103802`, M3 `26103803`. All new isolation presets deliberately retain this same S5 namespace, allowing active-position/history recognition when changing enabled methods. Unused S1–4 ownership magics are `26103810` through `26103813`. One controller lock per account/symbol prevents multiple VP charts from bypassing the shared S5 slot; method magic locks additionally prevent namespace collisions. Do not change magic namespaces or move/delete the lifecycle journal while positions are open. For same-symbol S1-5 coexistence, enable the desired strategies in one controller chart. Separate same-symbol controllers with alternative S1-4 magic namespaces are not a validated deployment: disabled strategies still participate in recovery, and a second chart must never be allowed to manage the same S5 magics or a different lifecycle journal. The independent-position acceptance tests exercise one controller on a hedging account, not multiple controller processes.

Daily entry counters count actual entry **orders**, deduplicating partial fills, per method per broker day. Maximum is five. Cooldown is 15 minutes after a **fully closed position**, not after submission, entry or partial close; broker history reconstructs it across restart/day boundaries. Position identifiers and actual entry ownership prove completed exits even if close comments/magics differ. Incomplete/reversed netting histories fail safe. Consumed setup globals, durable request metadata, broker order/position relations and startup replay prevent duplicate historical orders.

`InpRiskBrokerDay=true` is required when S5 is enabled. Old presets retain their existing New York risk-day default. The shared policy signature preserves its old value for the old mode; broker mode has a separate signature. Concurrent Scalping Bot charts on one account must use **identical shared risk policy settings**. This is one account budget, not an extra S5 allowance.

First attachment waits for a known day baseline. An observed broker boundary establishes it; across a quote/session closure, a previously observed **flat account with no intervening deal/funding** can carry its exactly known equity (maximum configured gap 96 hours). An unobserved boundary with open positions locks new entries because overnight account equity cannot be reliably invented. Start-of-day versus current account equity includes realized and floating P&L. Deposits, withdrawals and nontrade monetary adjustments lock the day for review rather than masking losses. Existing emergency/max-drawdown protections stay active. A risk-policy migration on an existing account requires stopping conflicting charts, being flat with no pending intent/orders, explicitly increasing ResetToken and applying consistent settings; the daily baseline lock remains. Reset is not permission to invent a day baseline.

## Presets, CSVs and research

New safe presets are `profiles/volume-profile-xauusd-m15-m5-balanced-demo.set`, `volume-profile-poc-bounce-only-demo.set`, `volume-profile-value-reversal-only-demo.set`, and `volume-profile-breakout-only-demo.set`. All disable S1–4 for isolation, keep signal-only on and real accounts blocked, and require broker-time verification. Use the balanced preset and independently enable other strategies for hedging comparisons; shared risk/position caps remain authoritative. Symbols must contain XAUUSD by default; custom naming requires an explicit reviewed override, not an assumed contract.

Local `MQL5/Files/ScalpingBot/` outputs: `*_vp_profiles.csv` contains active/reference dates, sessions, calculation parameters, M1 counts, raw coverage, volume, frozen levels, reference close/location, quality, weighted center and ties. `*_vp_signals.csv` contains per-method evaluations/transitions, IDs, states, raw flags, ATR/displacement/body/wick/BOS, candidate geometry and block/invalidation reasons. The existing version-2 `*_lifecycle.csv` remains backward compatible and records durable planned risk/RR, actual order/deal/position IDs, fills, partial exits, fees and net P&L; S5 profile/quality/context metadata is encoded in its existing structured `pivots` payload. Broker comments are not the attribution database.

`python tests/report_volume_profile.py --evidence <scenario-folder> --output <report-folder>` exports actual-deal CSV/JSON/Markdown metrics by method, side, confirmation, broker trading day, reference session and profile quality. It includes win/loss R, expectancy, net-position PF including actual fees, realized cash drawdown, holding time, loss streaks, blocked signals and invalidations. Realized R uses original requested monetary risk once per signal, including its deviation/cost allowance and requested-but-unfilled volume. It does not infer fills from accepted requests. Account floating-equity drawdown is separately taken from native tester results. Empty groups remain unavailable rather than invented win rates. Synthetic broker fixtures are not market performance.

Compile with `powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scalping-bot/compile.ps1`. Manual installation copies only this project's Expert and `Include/ScalpingBot` folder into the chosen demo terminal, then F7 and load the selected preset. No active-terminal deployment is automatic. Keep the two EA projects independent.

`tests/prepare_volume_profile_backtests.ps1` prepares native fixtures, frozen S1–4 regression inputs from the prior release, XAUUSD individual/all/combined-method runs, a cost-allowance stress run, and chronologically separated development (Aug 3–7), validation (Aug 10–14), and holdout (Aug 17–21) engineering slices. The portable runner retains exact inputs, raw CSV/logs, native reports and binary hashes. Broker UTC+3 is an explicit historical assumption. Test realistic commission schedules and spreads for the target broker, wide/narrow ranges, trends and high/low volatility across much longer samples before interpreting performance. No parameter optimization or assumption that M3 recovers earlier losses is part of this implementation. Final actual results and limitations are reported separately in VOLUME_PROFILE_VALIDATION.md.
